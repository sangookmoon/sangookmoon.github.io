Research

Hardware security · FPGA · Cryptographic hardware · Industrial IoT · Computer arithmetic

I work on cryptographic and security hardware for resource-constrained SoC-FPGAs, and on real-time signal-processing hardware for the industrial Internet of Things. Recent topics include lightweight cryptography (ASCON), homomorphic encryption, real-time anomaly-detection engines, and hardware for ultrasonic leak monitoring.
